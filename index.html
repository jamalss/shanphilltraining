Why train with us

Training built around real skills, not just certificates.

Every programme is designed to leave you able to do the job, not just talk about it — with a certification to back it up.

Practical, hands-on learning

Programmes are built around real tools, real procedures and real tasks in each trade or discipline.

Recognized certification

Certificate courses are recognized by the Ministry of Education in Saint Lucia, alongside CVQ and CAPE pathways.

A clear path forward

Each programme is structured with a defined duration and outcome, so you know what you’re working toward.

For individuals and teams

We train people building a career on their own, and organizations building capacity in their workforce.

Skills development beyond the classroom

Shan-Phill Training also provides training services for selected skills-development projects facilitated by NextGen Skills Foundation.

NextGen Skills Foundation is a separate nonprofit organization focused on skills development and community impact. Shan-Phill Training and NextGen Skills Foundation share a founder, but operate as two distinct organizations.
